# Answering tailoring quotes (ClothSeeker team)

Customers can start a design from a **photo of a dress** they like. When it needs changes our templates can't make (a slit, pleats, a different cut), they ask us for a price. Open **Admin → Quotes**. The dashboard tile "Quotes to answer" shows how many are waiting.

| Tab | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Needs reply** | New requests, the oldest deadline first, each with the time left. **Reply within 24 hours**; after that the request expires automatically and the customer is asked to request again. |
| **Quoted** | Prices you've sent that the customer hasn't paid yet. A price is valid for **7 days**. |
| **Closed** | Ordered, declined, expired or withdrawn by the customer. |

## Replying

The quote page shows the customer's design (frozen when they asked, so later edits don't change it), their **reference photo**, their **notes for the tailors**, and the design's colours and options. It shows no contact details.

- **Send price:** enter the price in taka **before delivery** (delivery is added at checkout) and say what it includes. The customer sees both.
- **Decline:** say briefly why we can't make it. The customer sees the reason.

You can reply only once, and only while the request is open. Every reply is recorded in the audit log.

## After the customer pays

The item appears in **Orders → To make** as **Accepted**. There's no separate acceptance step, because you already agreed the price. The spec sheet includes a **Tailoring request (quote)** section with the customer's notes and your reply, plus the reference photo.
